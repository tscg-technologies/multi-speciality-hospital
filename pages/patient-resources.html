Preparing for Your First Visit

To ensure a smooth registration process, please bring the following items to your first appointment:

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Health insurance card (if applicable)
  • List of current medications
  • Previous medical records and test results

Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete any necessary paperwork.
